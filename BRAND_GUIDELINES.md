# OpportunityKit Brand Guidelines

## Brand idea

OpportunityKit helps students move from scattered possibilities to a clear next step. The mark combines a starting point, a rising path, and a directional arrow to represent discovery, progress, and agency.

**Tagline:** Discover your next opportunity.

## Logo assets

- `assets/opportunitykit-logo-light.svg` — primary wordmark for light backgrounds.
- `assets/opportunitykit-logo-dark.svg` — wordmark for midnight or dark backgrounds.
- `assets/opportunitykit-mark.svg` — compact color mark for navigation, cards, and app surfaces.
- `assets/opportunitykit-mark-mono.svg` — monochrome version for print or constrained contexts.
- `assets/favicon.svg` — browser tab and app icon asset.

Keep the mark intact. Do not stretch, rotate, add effects, change the path geometry, or place it over a busy background. Maintain clear space of at least the mark's outer corner radius around the logo.

## Color system

- Midnight navy `#0B1933` — primary ink, dark hero surfaces, trust.
- Mint `#75E6C4` — action, progress, opportunity, positive emphasis.
- Soft violet `#C8B7FF` — secondary accent and directional detail.
- Off-white `#F7F7F2` — application canvas.
- Slate `#65738C` — supporting text and metadata.

Use navy for the strongest hierarchy and mint for focused actions or positive progress. Violet should remain an accent rather than the primary button color. Pair all status colors with text labels; never rely on color alone.

## Typography and UI

Use the existing system sans-serif stack for fast, local, dependable rendering. Headlines are compact and confident with negative letter spacing. Body copy stays readable and direct. Use medium borders, restrained shadows, and 10–18px radii. Prefer one strong surface over many decorative cards.

The product is intelligent but approachable. Avoid generic AI imagery, fake testimonials, unsupported metrics, noisy gradients, and decorative controls that do not perform a real action.

## Accessibility

Maintain visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, semantic status text, and reduced-motion support. Every listing should preserve its demo-data and source-verification context.
